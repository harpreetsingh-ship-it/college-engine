# Institutional Truth Boundaries

**Document:** Governance — Section 1
**Audience:** Institutional (external-facing — safe to share with districts)
**Status:** Complete

---

## Purpose

This document defines what the College Pathways Decision Engine does not do, will not claim, and is not designed to replace.

These boundaries are intentional. They exist to ensure the engine remains accurate, ethical, and suitable for institutional use.

---

## 1. No Predictions or Probabilities

The engine does not predict admissions outcomes. It does not:
- Estimate chances of acceptance
- Assign likelihood percentages
- Rank students competitively
- Forecast admissions decisions

**Why:** Admissions decisions are multi-factor, opaque, and institution-specific. Any probabilistic framing would introduce false precision and undermine trust.

The engine instead identifies:
- Pathways that are structurally viable
- Pathways that are structurally constrained
- Actions that still convert effort into outcomes

---

## 2. No Guarantees or Promises

The engine does not guarantee admission to any institution, system, or pathway. It does not:
- Promise acceptance
- Imply certainty
- Suggest inevitability of outcomes

**Why:** Admissions outcomes depend on factors beyond any single tool. The engine is designed to support planning, not to offer assurances.

---

## 3. No Replacement of Human Advising

The engine is an advisor-assist tool, not a counselor replacement. It does not:
- Make final decisions for families
- Substitute for counselor judgment
- Replace institutional advising processes
- Override school-specific guidance

**Why:** Human advisors provide contextual, emotional, and situational judgment that no rules engine should attempt to replicate.

The engine's role is to:
- Surface structural realities early
- Reduce misinformation
- Help advisors focus conversations on what matters

---

## 4. No Evaluation of Student Worth or Potential

The engine does not assess intelligence, motivation, character, long-term potential, or personal value. It evaluates pathway structure, not students.

**Why:** Academic trajectories are not proxies for human potential. The engine is intentionally constrained to operational planning, not personal judgment.

---

## 5. GPA Is Treated as a Structural Constraint, Not a Moral Judgment

The engine treats GPA as a primary structural input because admissions systems do. This does not imply:
- GPA defines the student
- GPA determines future success
- GPA cannot change over time

**Why:** Ignoring GPA's structural role would produce misleading guidance. Treating it honestly allows for better planning and fewer false levers.

---

## 6. Activities and Essays Are Amplifiers, Not Rescue Mechanisms

The engine does not present extracurriculars, essays, or testing as substitutes for unmet academic thresholds. It does not:
- Suggest activities can "offset" structural gaps
- Frame essays as rescue tools
- Imply late signals can override earlier records

**Why:** In most admissions systems, qualitative signals amplify viable paths rather than create them. Representing this accurately reduces false hope and wasted effort.

---

## 7. Equity Is Framed Through Access Patterns, Not Individual Labels

The engine uses concepts such as Access UC and Floor-Guarded UC to describe institutional behavior, not student categories. It does not:
- Label students as "fit" or "unfit"
- Assign identity-based classifications
- Encode demographic attributes

**Why:** These labels describe how institutions admit students historically, not who students are or deserve to be.

---

## 8. No Use of Sensitive Personal Data

The engine does not collect or use:
- Race or ethnicity
- Income or family finances
- Disability status
- Immigration status
- School or district identifiers
- Names or personally identifying information

**Why:** This ensures privacy, reduces bias risk, and simplifies institutional compliance.

---

## 9. No Hidden Optimization or Manipulative Framing

The engine does not:
- Optimize for engagement
- Nudge toward specific outcomes
- Steer users toward institutional interests
- Adjust guidance based on user behavior

Rules are deterministic, transparent, and fixed per version.

---

## 10. Boundaries Are a Feature, Not a Limitation

These constraints are deliberate design choices. They exist to ensure the engine:
- Remains trustworthy
- Avoids overreach
- Scales across institutions
- Supports advisors rather than undermines them

---

## Closing Statement

The College Pathways Decision Engine is designed to clarify what is structurally true and what is still actionable, without predicting outcomes or replacing human judgment.

Its value lies not in telling families what will happen — but in helping them plan responsibly within real constraints.
