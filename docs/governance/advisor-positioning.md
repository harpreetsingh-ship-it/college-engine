## Advisor-Assist, Not Advisor Replacement

---

## 4.1 Purpose of the Engine in an Advisory Context

The College Pathways Decision Engine is designed to support advisors, not replace them.

Its role is to:
- Surface structural realities early
- Reduce misinformation and false optionality
- Create a shared factual baseline for advisor–student conversations

The engine does not provide individualized counseling, emotional guidance, or final recommendations. Those functions remain the responsibility of trained advisors.

---

## 4.2 What the Engine Does Before the Advisor Steps In

The engine is intentionally positioned upstream of human advising. It performs three preparatory functions:

**1. Truth Stabilization**
Removes pathways that are no longer viable due to GPA or timing, preventing students from anchoring on outcomes that advisors cannot realistically support.

**2. Effort Re-allocation**
Highlights which actions still convert effort into outcomes — and which do not — reducing wasted time and misdirected stress.

**3. Language Normalization**
Provides consistent terminology (e.g., "locked," "viable," "high-ROI actions") so advisors do not have to repeatedly reframe expectations from scratch.

By the time an advisor engages, the conversation starts from shared reality, not aspiration mismatch.

---

## 4.3 What the Engine Explicitly Does Not Do

The engine does not:
- Replace counselor judgment
- Override institutional policy
- Make admissions decisions
- Rank students or campuses
- Predict acceptance outcomes
- Address personal, financial, or emotional factors
- Negotiate exceptions or special circumstances

Any output produced by the engine is contextual guidance, not a directive.

---

## 4.4 Advisor Authority Is Preserved by Design

Advisor authority is preserved in four ways:

**1. Structural Precedence**
Advisors retain discretion over how guidance is contextualized, especially in cases involving personal hardship, family constraints, institutional nuance, and non-standard transcripts.

**2. Interpretive Layering**
The engine explains why certain paths are constrained, but advisors interpret how a student should emotionally and strategically respond.

**3. No Hidden Logic**
Advisors can trace any output to a specific rule or gate. There is no "black box" authority competing with human expertise.

**4. Non-Finality**
The engine never produces a "final answer." It produces a planning frame.

---

## 4.5 Intended Use Models

Institutions may deploy the engine in multiple advisor-aligned ways:

**Pre-Advising Intake Tool**
Students complete the engine before meeting an advisor, reducing intake time spent correcting misconceptions.

**Expectation-Setting Companion**
Advisors use outputs during sessions to anchor difficult conversations in neutral, structural language.

**Equity Guardrail**
The engine ensures that all students receive the same baseline information, reducing variance driven by advisor workload or experience level.

In all cases, the engine is a decision-support artifact, not an advisor surrogate.

---

## 4.6 Institutional Positioning Statement (Recommended)

Institutions licensing the engine may use the following positioning language:

> "This tool is designed to support students and advisors by clarifying what options remain structurally viable and which actions are most effective at a given point in time. It does not replace individualized advising or institutional decision-making."

---

## Summary

The College Pathways Decision Engine:
- Clarifies reality
- Reduces misinformation
- Preserves human judgment
- Strengthens advisor conversations

It is a force multiplier for advising capacity, not a substitute for professional expertise.
