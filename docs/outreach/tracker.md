## Reply Handling Protocol

**Positive reply** ("interested", "let's talk"): Respond within 24 hours. Offer 2–3 specific time slots. Do not send tool link yet -- save for Zoom. Exception: practitioners (like Veronica Ortiz) get the link upfront.

**Lukewarm reply** ("send me more info"): Respond with 3 sentences + institutional brief. Ask if a 20-minute call makes sense after they review.

**Redirect** ("talk to X instead"): Thank them, ask for an intro or confirm you can reach out cold.

**No reply after nudge:** One final note 2 weeks after the nudge. Then close the thread -- no third attempt.
