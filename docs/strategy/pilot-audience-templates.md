## Template 3: Skeptical Parents

*Audience: Analytically-minded parents who will pressure-test the logic*
*Sent to: Krishna*

**Initial message:**

> I'm running a small pilot of a deterministic college planning engine I built — no predictions, no probabilities, no counseling.
>
> It encodes structural constraints (GPA, timing, systems) and outputs what's locked, what's still realistic, and where effort still changes outcomes.
>
> I'm not selling this or promoting it. I'm looking for smart parents to pressure-test the logic and language and tell me where it breaks or feels misleading.
>
> If you're open to sanity-checking something that deliberately avoids optimism, I'd value your reaction:
> https://harpreetsingh-ship-it.github.io/college-engine/

**Follow-up / feedback questions:**

> Thanks for taking a look. I'm not testing optimism or outcomes — I'm testing whether the logic and framing hold up under scrutiny.
>
> If you're willing, a few pointed questions:
> - Where did the reasoning feel solid or internally consistent?
> - Where did it feel fuzzy, hand-wavy, or incomplete?
> - Was there any conclusion you disagreed with — even if you understand why it was reached?
>
> I'm explicitly looking for failure modes and weak assumptions.

**Feedback received from this participant:**

> "I like the directional guidance. Make it less cluttered. Inputs / outputs — screen is very busy."
>
> "Output says Community College with CSU transfer — instead of UC/CSU Transfer. Transfer is also possible to OOS."
>
> "You can also consider structuring it differently, starting with inputs of preferences like $."
>
> "You can also include some links about Community College to UC Transfer as info along with Direct."
>
> "I am not sure how many people know this. You can provide financial info on tuition and options. Make it really like a counselor."
