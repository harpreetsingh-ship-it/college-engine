Institutional Pilot — Success Metrics Framework
Primary Objective

Validate that the College Pathways Structural Evaluation Framework produces reproducible structural guidance across counselors.

The pilot measures structural alignment, not admissions outcomes.

Primary Metric: Structural Alignment Rate

Definition:

Percentage of evaluations in which the counselor’s independent structural judgment is fully aligned with the framework’s output.

Calculation:

Fully Aligned Responses ÷ Total Logged Evaluations

Minimum Acceptable Threshold

75% Structural Alignment

Interpretation:

Below 60% → Structural instability; framework requires major revision.

60–74% → Insufficient consistency; targeted rule refinement required.

75–84% → Acceptable structural validity; refinement optional.

85%+ → Strong reproducibility; framework validated for scale.

Secondary Observations (Non-Threshold)

These are logged but do not determine pass/fail:

% Slightly Different

% Significantly Different

Common divergence themes (qualitative summary)

Alignment variation by GPA band

Alignment variation by counselor role

These insights inform refinement but do not redefine the primary threshold.

What Success Does Not Require

No improvement in admissions outcomes

No reduction in counselor workload

No measurable change in student satisfaction

No universal agreement across all cases

The pilot validates structural reproducibility only.

Pilot Completion Decision

At pilot conclusion, one of three decisions must be documented:

Proceed to broader district implementation

Refine framework rules and re-test

Discontinue framework use

Decision must reference measured alignment rate and divergence patterns.
